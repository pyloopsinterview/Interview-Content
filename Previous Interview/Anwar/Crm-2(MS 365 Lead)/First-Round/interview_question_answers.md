## 2️⃣ Power Platform
### Question: What is Power Apps?

**Answer:**  
**Power Apps is Microsoft’s low-code application development platform** for creating secure, scalable business apps for web, mobile, and tablets. It enables organizations to replace manual or Excel-driven processes by building modern, automated applications that connect with **Dataverse, Dynamics 365, SQL, SharePoint**, and 1,000+ data connectors.

There are **three types of Power Apps**:  
1. **Canvas Apps** – fully customizable UI for flexible design  
2. **Model-Driven Apps** – data-driven apps built on Dataverse with standard UI  
3. **Power Pages** – external portals for customer/vendor interactions  

Power Apps supports **Power Automate, AI Builder, role-based security, and Azure integration**, enabling enterprises to rapidly digitize and scale their operations with lower development cost.

## 3️⃣ CRM Knowledge
### Question: What is CRM?

**Answer:**  
**CRM (Customer Relationship Management)** is a business system used to **manage customer data, sales processes, service interactions, and marketing lifecycle** from a single platform. It provides a **360° customer view** to improve engagement, retention, and operational efficiency. CRM enables automation of **lead management, opportunity tracking, case management**, and analytics to enhance business growth.

### Question: What is Dynamics 365?

**Answer:**  
**Dynamics 365 is Microsoft’s unified cloud platform** that combines both **CRM and ERP** functionalities for automating **Sales, Service, Field Operations, Marketing, Finance, and Supply Chain** processes. It is built on **Azure and Dataverse**, allowing seamless integration with **Power Apps, Power BI, Teams, Outlook, SAP, Salesforce** and more.

It provides **AI insights (Copilot), scalable cloud architecture**, and deep automation capabilities to support large enterprise digital transformation.

## 5️⃣ Data Migration Knowledge
### Question: What tools and strategies have you used for data migration?

**Answer:**  
I follow a structured **ETL approach** with focus on **data accuracy and integrity**, using these tools:

• **KingswaySoft (SSIS)** – primary tool for large volumes and ERP integration  
• **Azure Data Factory** – pipeline orchestration and hybrid dataset migration  
• **SQL Server** – staging, cleansing, transformation, deduplication  
• **Dynamics Import Wizard** – controlled or initial configuration data loads  
• **Power Automate & Web APIs** – incremental or specialized migrations  

Strategies include:  
• **Data modeling and dependency mapping**  
• **Pre-validation in staging**  
• **Sequencing: Accounts → Contacts → Transactions → Activities**  
• **Multiple dry runs before cutover**  
• **Reconciliation, audit logs & business sign-off**  

This ensures secure, compliant, and error-free migration in production environments.
