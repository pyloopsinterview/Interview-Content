## 7. What is the difference between AX and Dynamics 365 F&O?

AX was primarily an on-premise ERP system. Dynamics 365 F&O is the
cloud-based evolution of AX. It offers: - Cloud architecture - Better
integration with Power Platform and Azure - Modern UI and enhanced
security - Improved scalability and performance
