## ✅ **Interview Questions & Model Answers (Markdown Format)**

### **1. Why are you looking for a new opportunity?**

I’m looking for a role where I can continue growing technically, work on meaningful product-driven challenges, and contribute more toward architecture and end-to-end ownership.

### **2. Have you worked with Agile teams before?**

**Answer:**
Yes. I’ve worked in Agile environments where we followed Scrum practices — sprint planning, daily stand-ups, reviews, and retros. I enjoy Agile because it promotes collaboration, fast feedback cycles, and continuous improvement. Agile helps keep delivery focused and transparent, which aligns with how I like to work.

---

### **3. What strengths do you think you bring to a team/company?**

**Answer:**

- Strong end-to-end ownership
- Ability to break complex problems into manageable steps
- Clear and proactive communication
- Focus on delivering business value, not just code
- Collaborative mindset and willingness to support teammates

I combine technical depth with teamwork and product understanding.

---

### **4. What type of culture do you thrive best in?**

**Answer:**
A collaborative, transparent, growth-focused culture.
I thrive in teams where:

- Knowledge sharing is encouraged
- People support each other
- Feedback is constructive
- Innovation and ownership are valued

I like a culture where we celebrate wins together and learn from failures.

---

### **5. What key points of communication do you expect from your team?**

**Answer:**

- Clear requirements and acceptance criteria
- Expected timelines and priority alignment
- Open feedback around design decisions
- Regular sync on blockers and risks

I also appreciate proactive updates in standups and clarity through tools like Jira, Slack, and Confluence.

---

### **6. How do you handle sudden context-switching between tasks/projects?**

**Answer:**
I manage this by:

- Breaking work into small milestones
- Maintaining clear notes and to-do lists
- Prioritizing based on urgency & business impact
- Communicating if switching may affect timelines

I stay adaptable yet structured to avoid losing momentum.

---

### **7. How do you balance your own work while helping teammates?**

**Answer:**
I follow a prioritization approach — I focus on completing my critical tasks while also being available for support. I schedule help windows, do efficient code reviews, and prefer async communication when possible.
If someone is truly blocked, I assist immediately — team success comes first.

---

### **8. When you're unfamiliar with a story or technology, how do you approach it?**

**Answer:**

- Start with requirement deep-dive and understanding the business context
- Review documentation or similar code paths
- Break down tasks into learning + execution steps
- Ask clarifying questions early to avoid rework
- Build small proof-of-concepts if needed

I believe in learning fast by combining self-research + collaboration.

---

**Answer:**
Both, depending on the situation.

- Clear stories → I prefer working independently for deep focus
- New or complex problems → Pair programming helps align ideas and accelerate learning

Balance of independence and teamwork creates better outcomes.
