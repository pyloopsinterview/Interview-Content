# Interview Questions Asked Today

1. Do you prefer contract roles, contract-to-hire, or direct hire positions?

2. Since you are a U.S. citizen, would you be open to contract-to-hire opportunities if they transition to full-time later?

3. How long would you typically be willing to stay on contract before moving to a full-time role?

4. Can you describe your main responsibilities and primary focus on your most recent project?

5. How much experience do you have with modern Java technologies such as Java 21, Kotlin, and newer JVM versions compared to the traditional Java stack?

6. When evaluating new opportunities, what factors do you typically focus on?

7. How important is a job title like Senior or Lead to you when considering a new role?

8. Are there any previous projects you would like to highlight, including specific technologies or notable work?

9. Do you have a preference for the balance between new development and maintenance work, or are you flexible?

10. Are you open to 100% remote roles, and are you also open to roles based in Texas?

11. How quickly would you be able to start a new position?

12. Do you handle pay directly, or does your employer or vendor manage that?

13. What corporate-to-corporate hourly rate are you targeting?

14. Are there any specific industries, technologies, or focus areas you are targeting in your next opportunity?
