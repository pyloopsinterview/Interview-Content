# Interview Questions Asked Today -- With Sample Answers

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Do you prefer contract roles, contract-to-hire, or direct hire positions?

**Answer:**\
I am open to all three options depending on the opportunity. My primary
focus is on the role responsibilities, technology stack, and long-term
growth rather than the employment type.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Since you are a U.S. citizen, would you be open to contract-to-hire opportunities if they transition to full-time later?

**Answer:**\
Yes, I am open to contract-to-hire opportunities, especially if there is
a clear path to full-time employment based on performance and business
needs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. How long would you typically be willing to stay on contract before moving to a full-time role?

**Answer:**\
Typically, 6 to 12 months is reasonable. However, I am flexible
depending on project requirements and performance expectations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
