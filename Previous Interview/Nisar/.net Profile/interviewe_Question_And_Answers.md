## 2. What Is QPTM (Quorum) and How Does It Work?

**QPTM (Quorum Production Transaction Management)** is a comprehensive production data management solution used in the Oil & Gas industry.
It handles the entire **production lifecycle** — from field data capture to allocation, balancing, validation, and reporting.

It integrates with **field systems, SCADA, and accounting platforms** to centralize and standardize production data. QPTM applies **allocation rules and balancing logic** to ensure accuracy and traceability of production volumes across wells and pipelines.

**Key Features:**

* Centralized production data capture and validation.
* Automated allocation and balancing.
* Integration with SCADA and enterprise systems.
* Built-in audit trails and exception management.
* API-based connectivity for analytics and reporting tools.
