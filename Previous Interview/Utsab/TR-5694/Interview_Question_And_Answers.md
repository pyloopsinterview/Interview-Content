# Utsab Interview Q&A Preparation

## 1. Tell me more about your background and what you are currently working on.

Hi, my name is Utsab Poudel. I have about 13 years of experience in
software engineering, mainly focused on backend development using Python
and building scalable cloud-based systems.

Currently, I'm working at Health First as a Lead Python Backend
Developer. My primary responsibility is designing and developing backend
services that process large volumes of healthcare and financial data.
Because we operate in a regulated healthcare environment, our systems
must meet strict requirements around security, compliance, data
integrity, and auditability.

One of the main platforms I work on is a claims processing and financial
reconciliation system used by internal finance and operations teams. The
platform processes millions of healthcare claim transactions, validates
them against different business rules and compliance policies, and
ensures that payments and financial records are accurate.

From a technical standpoint, I design and develop Python-based
microservices that handle claim validation, transaction processing, and
audit tracking. These services are containerized using Docker and
managed through Kubernetes, and our infrastructure runs on Google Cloud
Platform.

To efficiently handle large transaction volumes, we built an
event-driven architecture where services communicate through
asynchronous messaging pipelines. This allows the system to scale
efficiently while maintaining reliable processing and strong audit
trails.

Overall, my work focuses on building reliable backend systems, designing
scalable microservices architectures, and deploying cloud-native
applications using Python and modern cloud technologies.
