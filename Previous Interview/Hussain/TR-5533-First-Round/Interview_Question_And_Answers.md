## 3. So you're more on the patient health side, like analyzing doctor notes?

**Answer:**

No, my work is not on the clinical or diagnosis side.

I focus on the security and governance layer. I build systems that control who can access patient data, not systems that analyze medical data.

For example, we ensure only authorized doctors, nurses, or billing staff have appropriate access based on their roles. If someone changes roles or leaves the company, their access is automatically updated or revoked.

The AI component we built analyzes access behavior — not medical data — to detect risky access patterns or abnormal privilege escalation.
