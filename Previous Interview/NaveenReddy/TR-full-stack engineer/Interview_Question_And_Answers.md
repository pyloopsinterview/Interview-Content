## 4. Describe a cloud-based system you designed on AWS and your role

**Answer:**

My role was the primary designer and owner of the system, responsible for architecture, implementation, CI/CD, monitoring, and production support.

## 5. How do you decide between Lambda and container-based services?

**Answer:**  
I use Lambda for short-lived, event-driven, stateless workloads with unpredictable traffic. I choose containers when workloads are long-running, require custom dependencies, or need more control over runtime and scaling.

---

## 6. How do you design systems for reliability and failure handling?

**Answer:**  
I assume failures will happen, decouple components, implement retries and idempotency, add timeouts and backpressure, design strong observability, and ensure runbooks and rollback strategies are in place.
