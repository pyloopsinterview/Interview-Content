# Dynamics 365 Finance – Interview Questions & Answers

## Q1. Have you been using Business Central or other Dynamics 365 products?
**A:**  
Yes. I’ve been working extensively with Dynamics 365 Business Central, and I also regularly use other Dynamics 365 Finance components along with the Power Platform. My day-to-day work includes Business Central configurations, finance processes, integrations, workflows, and reporting, so I’m actively using the broader Dynamics 365 ecosystem.

---

## Q2. This role is a mix of high-level architecture and hands-on development. How do you handle that balance?
**A:**  
I usually start by defining the overall architecture and technical direction, making sure it aligns with business and enterprise standards. After that, I stay hands-on by building custom extensions, integrations, workflows, and reviewing code. I like being involved end to end so I can ensure the solution is scalable, clean, and actually works in practice.

---

## Q3. Which programming languages do you use most when customizing Dynamics 365?
**A:**  
I primarily use C# and .NET for integrations and custom services. For Business Central, I work with AL for extensions and reports. I also use JavaScript for UI-level customizations and Power FX within the Power Platform when needed.

---

## Q4. Which Dynamics 365 Finance modules are you most comfortable with?
**A:**  
My strongest experience is in finance modules, especially General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Procure-to-Pay, Fixed Assets, Project Accounting, and Order-to-Cash. My deepest expertise is in AP and AR, where I’ve done a lot of customization, workflow design, and integrations.

---

## Q5. What experience do you have with forms customization?
**A:**  
I’ve customized AP and AR forms by adding custom fields, extending standard entities, implementing validations, and applying role-based security. These changes support business rules like approval controls, payment logic, and customer credit checks.

---

## Q6. What is your experience with workflows?
**A:**  
I’ve designed and implemented multi-level workflows for vendor invoices, payment journals, and customer approvals. These workflows include conditional routing based on amount, cost center, legal entity, and financial dimensions, along with escalation and audit tracking.

---

## Q7. What experience do you have with dashboards and reports?
**A:**  
I’ve built role-based dashboards using Power BI and embedded them into Dynamics 365 for AP and AR teams. These dashboards provide visibility into invoice aging, overdue receivables, cash flow, and approval backlogs. I’ve also customized standard financial reports and created custom Power BI reports for leadership and audit purposes.

---

## Q8. What systems have you integrated with Dynamics 365 Finance?
**A:**  
I’ve integrated D365 Finance with legacy ERP systems, vendor management systems, banking and payment platforms, and internal enterprise applications. The integrations handled vendor data, customer data, invoices, payments, GL balances, and journal entries.

---

## Q9. What tools do you use for integrations?
**A:**  
I use REST and OData APIs, custom data entities, and .NET/C# services. For scalable integrations, I use Azure Functions, Logic Apps, and Service Bus. I also use Power Automate for workflow-based integrations and Dataverse and Power BI for analytics.

---

## Q10. What is your process for data migration?
**A:**  
I start with data assessment and mapping, followed by data cleansing and validation. I then migrate data in phases using the Data Management Framework with standard and custom data entities. I run multiple mock migrations, perform reconciliations, and validate balances before cutover and go-live.

---

## Q11. What tools do you prefer for data migration?
**A:**  
My primary tools are the Data Management Framework, Excel and CSV templates, configuration packages, and custom data entities. For complex scenarios, I also use custom scripts and validation frameworks to ensure accuracy and audit compliance.

---
