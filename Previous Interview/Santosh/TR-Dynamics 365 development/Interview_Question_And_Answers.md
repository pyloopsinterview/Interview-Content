# Interview Questions and Answers

## Architecture & Background

**Q: Can you talk through your experience and highlight areas where you’ve been exposed to the kind of work we’re doing so you can continue to support it?**  
A: Yes. I’ve worked on large, enterprise Dynamics 365 platforms that are already live and heavily used. My focus has been on supporting, stabilizing, and enhancing existing systems—especially around integrations, custom code, data models, and Azure middleware—so I’m very comfortable stepping in and continuing support rather than starting from scratch.

**Q: Have you worked in older, long-established organizations that are modernizing their systems?**  
A: Yes. Most of my recent experience has been in mature organizations with legacy systems that needed modernization. I’ve worked in environments where older platforms coexisted with newer cloud-based solutions, and the goal was to modernize incrementally without disrupting the business.

**Q: Have you supported systems where leadership shifted the company toward newer technologies?**  
A: Absolutely. I’ve seen that shift firsthand—new leadership driving initiatives around cloud adoption, data platforms, and Dynamics 365. I’ve supported those transitions by helping redesign integrations, data flows, and CRM capabilities in a practical, phased approach.

## Azure & Integrations

**Q: Have you worked extensively with Azure services as middleware for integrations?**  
A: Yes. Azure is the core integration layer in my current role, and I use it for most system-to-system integrations.

**Q: Have you set up and managed Azure resources yourself?**  
A: Yes. I’ve set up and configured Azure Functions, Service Bus, Storage Accounts, Azure SQL, app registrations, managed identities, and access policies.

**Q: Can you give an example where you built or extended an MVP using Azure services?**  
A: Yes. After an MVP went live, I helped extend it by onboarding new data sources using Blob Storage, Azure Functions, and Service Bus, without disrupting existing integrations.

**Q: Have you worked with blob storage–based ingestion patterns?**  
A: Yes. I’ve implemented patterns where business users or upstream systems drop files into Blob Storage, which are then validated, processed, and loaded into Dynamics.

**Q: Have you supported SFTP-based integrations?**  
A: Yes. I’ve worked with SFTP integrations for scheduled and batch-based data exchanges.

**Q: Have you worked with API-based and third-party integrations?**  
A: Yes. I’ve designed and supported both real-time API integrations and third-party systems for lead enrichment and data validation.

**Q: Have you supported and maintained a large number of integration patterns?**  
A: Yes. I’ve supported dozens of integrations simultaneously, including monitoring, troubleshooting, and enhancements.
